Privacy Policy

Note on this version: This privacy policy was drafted based on the legal requirements of the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG) and follows the structure and tone of official FMH model data-protection documents. The specific FMH model privacy policy for medical-practice websites was not available at the time of drafting; this text will be reconciled with it once available. This privacy policy does not replace a legal review before go-live.

2. Purpose of processing

Personal data collected via this website are used exclusively to respond to contact enquiries and to provide general information about the practice and its services. The website itself is not used for medical treatment, diagnosis or documentation.

3. Categories of data processed

When you visit the website, the hosting provider technically processes server log data (including IP address, time of access, page requested). If you voluntarily fill in the contact form, the following details are additionally collected: name, email address, optionally a phone number, and the message you enter. The pre-check (risk self-assessment) runs entirely anonymously and locally in your browser; your answers are not transmitted to this website or to any server.

4. Legal basis

Your personal data are processed on the basis of Art. 31 para. 1 revDSG, to fulfil an existing or prospective contractual relationship with you, or to safeguard the practice's legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Where consent is required in an individual case (e.g. the consent checkbox on the contact form), that consent forms the legal basis.

5. Recipients of data

Data entered via the contact form are transmitted solely to the practice, specifically to the encrypted HIN mailbox paul.georg@hin.ch as the sole recipient. Depending on the technical setup of the form at the time of your visit, this happens either directly via your own email program (your details are then not transmitted to any server of this website) or via a form service provider acting as a processor, used solely to forward the data to the address above. Data are not passed on to any other third parties, in particular not for advertising or analytics purposes. Form data are not cached on any server operated by this website itself — the website is static and has no database of its own.

6. Retention period

Server log data held by the hosting provider are automatically deleted after its usual, short retention periods. Enquiries submitted via the contact form are kept only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry. Data collected in the course of actual treatment at the practice (medical records) are subject to the statutory retention periods described in the separate patient information sheet you receive at the practice.

7. Your rights

Under the revDSG, you have the right to obtain information about the data processed about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data deleted, and to receive or transfer your data in a common format, insofar as no statutory retention obligations apply. To exercise these rights, please use the contact details below.

8. Contact for privacy enquiries

For questions about data protection or to exercise your rights, please contact us by phone at +41 43 234 55 55 or by post at the practice address above.

9. Medical professional confidentiality

Independently of this privacy policy, Dr. med. Paul Georg, as a physician, is bound by medical professional confidentiality under Art. 321 of the Swiss Criminal Code. Information entrusted in the course of treatment is subject to this special statutory protection.

10. Contact form — important note

Please do not submit any medical data, findings or symptom descriptions via the contact form. For this, please use the phone contact or a secure HIN email. The contact form is intended solely for general, non-medical enquiries.

11. Hosting

This website is operated as a static, serverless site with no database of its own. The final hosting location for production use has not yet been definitively decided and will be added here before the website goes live.

12. Cookies

This website currently uses no cookies. No statistics/analytics cookies and no marketing or tracking cookies are used. Fonts are served locally by this website rather than loaded from an external provider, so no connection to third-party font servers is made when a page loads. Only your choice on the cookie notice banner (seen/not seen) is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) — this is technically not a cookie, and no data is transmitted to any server.
