2. Purpose of processing

Personal data collected via this website are used exclusively to respond to contact enquiries and to provide general information about the practice and its services. The website itself is not used for medical treatment, diagnosis or documentation.

3. Categories of data processed

When you visit the website, the hosting provider technically processes server log data (including IP address, time of access, page requested). If you voluntarily fill in the contact form, the following details are additionally collected: name, email address, optionally a phone number, and the message you enter. The pre-check (risk self-assessment) runs entirely anonymously and locally in your browser; your answers are not transmitted to this website or to any server.

4. Legal basis

Your personal data are processed on the basis of Art. 31 para. 1 revDSG, to fulfil an existing or prospective contractual relationship with you, or to safeguard the practice's legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Where consent is required in an individual case (e.g. the consent checkbox on the contact form), that consent forms the legal basis.

5. Recipients of data

Data entered via the contact form are transmitted solely to the practice, specifically to the encrypted HIN mailbox clinicselnau@hin.ch as the sole recipient. Depending on the technical setup of the form at the time of your visit, this happens either directly via your own email program (your details are then not transmitted to any server of this website) or via a form service provider acting as a processor, used solely to forward the data to the address above. Registrations for the opening reception go to paul.georg@hin.ch. The registration form for patients of MediX Praxis Altstetten opens a prepared email to clinicselnau@hin.ch in your own email program; the details are not transmitted to any server of this website. Data are not passed on to any other third parties, in particular not for advertising or analytics purposes. Form data are not cached on any server operated by this website itself — the website is static and has no database of its own.

6. Retention period

Server log data held by the hosting provider are automatically deleted after its usual, short retention periods. Enquiries submitted via the contact form are kept only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry. Data collected in the course of actual treatment at the practice (medical records) are subject to the statutory retention periods described in the separate patient information sheet you receive at the practice.

7. Your rights

Under the revDSG, you have the right to obtain information about the data processed about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data deleted, and to receive or transfer your data in a common format, insofar as no statutory retention obligations apply. To exercise these rights, please use the contact details below.

8. Contact for privacy enquiries

For questions about data protection or to exercise your rights, please contact us by phone at +41 43 234 55 55, by email at clinicselnau@hin.ch or by post at the practice address above.

9. Medical professional confidentiality

Independently of this privacy policy, Dr. med. Paul J. Georg, as a physician, is bound by medical professional confidentiality under Art. 321 of the Swiss Criminal Code. Information entrusted in the course of treatment is subject to this special statutory protection.

10. Contact form — important note

Please do not submit any medical data, findings or symptom descriptions via the contact form. For this, please use the phone contact or a secure HIN email to clinicselnau@hin.ch. The contact form is intended solely for general, non-medical enquiries.

11. Hosting

This website is operated as a static, serverless site with no database of its own.

12. Cookies

This website itself currently uses no cookies. No statistics/analytics cookies and no marketing or tracking cookies are used. Fonts are served locally by this website rather than loaded from an external provider, so no connection to third-party font servers is made when a page loads. Only your choice on the cookie notice banner (seen/not seen) and the fact that you have already seen the practice-opening popup are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) — this is technically not a cookie, and no data is transmitted to any server. Exception: if you load the Google map on the home page, Google may set its own cookies (see section 13).

13. Google Maps (map on click)

On the home page we show the location of Clinic Selnau in Zurich district 2 with a map from Google Maps. The map is only loaded when you click «Load map». Before that, no connection to Google is made.

After the click, your browser connects to Google servers. Google receives technical data such as your IP address, the page visited and browser and device information. Google may set cookies and may transfer the data to countries outside Switzerland, for example the USA. We have no influence on this processing by Google.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by clicking «Load map». It applies only to the page view concerned. If you do not load the map, there is no disadvantage to you: the address is shown right next to it. Further information is available in Google's privacy policy.

The link «Clinic Selnau on Google» opens a Google page. Data is only transmitted once you click the link.
