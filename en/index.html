Risk Triage

How high is your skin cancer risk?

Thirteen questions about your history, skin type and UV exposure. The result places you within a body of research — it is not a diagnosis.

Based on validated risk models (incl. QSkin/MP16) · Does not replace a medical diagnosis.

Risk score

 points
Not a diagnostic tool. This pre-check is a statistical orientation tool. The rates referred to come from epidemiological studies of risk groups and say nothing about actual disease in an individual case: a low result does not rule out melanoma, and a high one is not a diagnosis. Only a medical examination can confirm or exclude skin cancer.

Surgery and Measurability

How do you make medical quality visible?

Dr. med. Paul Georg studied medicine at Hannover Medical School and carried out his doctoral research at St. Thomas' Hospital / King's College London — developing a method for objectively measuring outcomes of cleft lip and palate surgery, using 3D photography and a self-defined facial symmetry index.

Years in surgery followed at Kantonsspital Münsterlingen (general surgery, intensive care, hand and plastic surgery), along with a placement abroad at New Somerset Hospital in Cape Town.

Dermatology became his professional home: further training at the Skin and Laser Centre Weinfelden, the University Dermatology Clinic at Inselspital Bern, and the Bellevue Vein Clinic Kreuzlingen. As head of the wound care clinic at Spital Affoltern am Albis, he developed a semi-automated algorithm for digital wound measurement, published in the Journal of Clinical Medicine in 2024. He is a founding member and board member of the EWMA European Young Wound Experts Group.

2026: Board examination in Dermatology and Venereology (FMH) and founding of Dermetrics.
