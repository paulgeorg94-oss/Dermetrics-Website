Clinic Selnau

About Clinic Selnau

Clinic Selnau is a specialised group practice in the centre of Zurich: each physician deliberately focuses on one clearly defined specialty rather than covering the full spectrum. This focus shapes how an indication is assessed and a procedure is planned, always with the goal of respecting each person's individual anatomy and changing only what genuinely benefits from it — to a high specialist standard.

Surgery and Measurability

How do you make medical quality visible?

Dr. med. Paul J. Georg studied medicine at Hannover Medical School and carried out his doctoral research at St. Thomas' Hospital / King's College London — developing a method for objectively measuring outcomes of cleft lip and palate surgery, using 3D photography and a self-defined facial symmetry index.

Years in surgery followed at Kantonsspital Münsterlingen (general surgery, intensive care, hand and plastic surgery), along with a placement abroad at New Somerset Hospital in Cape Town.

Dermatology became his professional home: further training at the Skin and Laser Centre Weinfelden, the University Dermatology Clinic at Inselspital Bern, and the Bellevue Vein Clinic Kreuzlingen. As head of the wound care clinic at Spital Affoltern am Albis, he developed a semi-automated algorithm for digital wound measurement, published in the Journal of Clinical Medicine in 2024. He is a founding member and board member of the EWMA European Young Wound Experts Group.

2026: Board examination in Dermatology and Venereology (FMH) and founding of Dermetrics.

Skin cancer and tumour surgery

  • We treat skin cancer and perform skin cancer screening (full-body dermoscopy, 3D scan).
  • We treat basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma and actinic keratoses.
  • We treat all kinds of skin tumours surgically in our certified practice operating room.
  • We treat skin tumours with micrographically controlled surgery (Mohs surgery).
  • We treat moles when the findings are conspicuous (monitoring and excision).

Wounds and scars

  • We treat chronic and complex wounds, including with ECMP.
  • We treat scars: acne scars, surgical scars, hypertrophic scars and keloids.
  • We also treat scars with laser (Mosaic 3D Erbium).

Chronic skin conditions

  • We treat psoriasis.
  • We treat hidradenitis suppurativa (acne inversa).
  • We treat acne and rosacea.
  • We treat melasma and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.

Aesthetic dermatology

  • We treat benign skin lesions for aesthetic reasons (for example fibromas, warts, seborrhoeic keratoses).
  • We treat sun-related skin ageing (solar elastosis) and advise on skin care.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which treatments does Dermetrics offer in Zurich Selnau/Enge?

We treat skin tumours and perform skin cancer screening. We also treat wounds, scars, psoriasis, hidradenitis suppurativa (acne inversa), acne, rosacea, melasma and benign skin lesions for aesthetic reasons.

Where is the practice?

Clinic Selnau is at Brandschenkestrasse 45, 8002 Zürich, in district 2 (Selnau/Enge). Further locations are Affoltern am Albis (Obstgartenstrasse 3, 8910) and Zurich-Altstetten (Hohlstrasse 556, 8048, 3D analysis).

When is Clinic Selnau open?

Consultations are on Mondays from 10:00 to 18:00. The practice opens on 7 December 2026.

How does a skin cancer screening work?

During the screening we examine the skin with full-body dermoscopy. 3D scans are taken at the Zurich-Altstetten location, and you receive the results by email within 72 hours. The online Pre-Check is a statistical orientation tool, not a diagnosis.

Are operations performed directly in the practice?

Yes. We carry out dermatologic surgery such as the removal of skin tumours in our certified practice operating room.

Do you offer micrographically controlled surgery (Mohs surgery)?

Yes. In micrographically controlled surgery the tumour is removed layer by layer and the margins are examined under the microscope for tumour-free edges before the defect is closed. Whether this procedure is suitable for your findings is decided in the medical assessment.

Do you work with plastic surgery?

Yes. For complex findings or larger defects we work in an interdisciplinary team with plastic surgery at Clinic Selnau (Dr. med. Simon Zimmermann).

How are chronic wounds treated?

We treat chronic and complex wounds based on experience from the Advanced Wound Therapies wound centre at Inselspital Bern and the wound clinic at Spital Affoltern am Albis, including the use of ECMP (extracellular-matrix-like products).

How do I book an appointment?
