Solar Elastosis

Skin thickening caused by sun damage

Not skin cancer itself, but an important marker of elevated skin cancer risk.

At Dermetrics, the practice of Dr. med. Paul J. Georg at Clinic Selnau in Zurich Selnau/Enge, we treat sun-related skin ageing (solar elastosis). All services

Responsible: Dr. med. Paul J. Georg, specialist in Dermatology and Venereology · Last reviewed: 25 September 2026

In short: Solar elastosis is a thickening and coarsening of the skin caused by years of UV exposure, producing thickened, coarse-pored, yellowish-sallow skin with deep wrinkles on sun-exposed areas. It is not itself a skin cancer precursor, but indicates a significant sun-exposure history and is linked to a markedly elevated risk of non-melanoma skin cancer.

Overview

Solar elastosis is a thickening and coarsening of the skin caused by years of UV exposure: the elastic fibres of the dermis degenerate and are deposited in a disorganised form. Clinically, this presents as thickened, coarse-pored, often yellowish-sallow skin with deep wrinkles, usually on chronically sun-exposed areas such as the face, neck and forearms.

Solar elastosis itself is not a skin cancer precursor. However, it indicates a significant history of sun exposure and is associated with a markedly elevated risk of non-melanoma skin cancer (NMSC): tissue samples from basal cell and squamous cell carcinomas show a three- to four-fold increase in elastotic tissue compared with normal sun-exposed skin.

Treatment Options

Aesthetic-cosmetic skin renewal

At this early stage, no medical intervention is yet necessary. Purely aesthetic-cosmetic skin-rejuvenation measures, such as laser resurfacing, are sufficient to improve skin structure and quality.

Preventive sun-protection protocol

In addition, a consistent sun-protection protocol of sunscreen, niacinamide and retinoic acid helps limit further photodamage and reduce the long-term risk of downstream damage.

Because solar elastosis reflects the pre-existing skin damage from which actinic keratoses and non-melanoma skin cancer can later develop, treating it is deliberately the first step of skin cancer prevention within the Dermetrics concept, long before medical treatment becomes necessary.
