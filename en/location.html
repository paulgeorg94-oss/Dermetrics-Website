Location: Clinic Selnau in Zurich Enge, district 2

Dermetrics – Dermatologie Clinic Selnau. Dr. med. Paul J. Georg, specialist in dermatology and venereology.

In brief: The practice is located at Clinic Selnau, Brandschenkestrasse 45, 8002 Zürich, in Zurich Enge (district 2, Selnau), near Enge railway station and Paradeplatz. Consultations take place on Mondays from 10:00 to 18:00. The practice opens on 7 December 2026.
